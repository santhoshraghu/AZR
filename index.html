Leadership

Founder

Anwar Rohan
Founder | Chief Executive Officer

Mr. Rohan is a strategic leader and innovator in the retail energy space, with over 15 years of experience driving growth, building platforms, and unlocking value at the intersection of pricing, technology, and market strategy. His work spans electric and natural gas services across deregulated markets in the U.S., where he has led initiatives in pricing, structuring, trading, M&A, and software development.

At the core of his mission is a commitment to demystifying electricity market pricing and fostering greater transparency, access, and agility in how energy is priced, consumed, and managed. He envisions a modern grid that operates in synergy with real-time data, renewable energy, and smart technologies and has spent his career building the tools and strategies to make that vision real.

Anwar has led teams in scaling startups, launching customer-centric energy platforms, and delivering first-of-their-kind digital experiences that empower users to interact directly with market-based electricity pricing. His approach blends deep domain knowledge with a forward-looking mindset grounded in data integrity, technical fluency, and systemic thinking all aimed at shaping a smarter, more responsive energy future.
